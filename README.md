## The image

A newspaper-style infographic, 1024×1024, with five panels under the
headline "WORLD NEWS — 19 NOVEMBER 2026":

1. **XI–TRUMP–PUTIN SUMMIT IN SHENZHEN** — Trump meets Xi Jinping and
   Vladimir Putin together in Shenzhen during APEC, the first three-way
   summit, with nuclear arms control, Ukraine and the Iran conflict on
   the agenda.
2. **GULF STRIKES — TANKER HIT IN HORMUZ** — Iran and the US trade
   strikes across the Gulf; Iran fires at a US air base in Kuwait and
   Bahrain, the US hits a Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm
   Island, and a tanker is struck in the Strait of Hormuz.
3. **GTA VI LAUNCHES WORLDWIDE** — Grand Theft Auto VI begins its
   global rollout, with gamers entering Vice City on launch day.
4. **VOYAGER 1 REACHES A LIGHT-DAY** — NASA's Voyager 1 probe is
   believed to have reached a light-day from Earth.
5. **EU PLANS €45 BILLION UKRAINE LOAN** — The EU proposes a €45
   billion loan to Ukraine, to be repaid after Russia pays reparations.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13) deterministically copies the bytes of `entry.png`
(shipped in the repo root) verbatim to `/output/entry.png`. No network,
no randomness, no time, no environment — two runs produce identical bytes.
